# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are recruiters, hiring managers, and engineering leaders evaluating Rifki Fajar Ramadhani for full-stack engineering roles. They need to understand his experience, technical range, production impact, and fit quickly enough to decide whether to begin an interview or job-opportunity conversation.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents evidence of Rifki's work as a full-stack software engineer and gives hiring teams a direct path from evaluation to contact. Success means turning credible review of his experience, projects, skills, and resume into relevant interview and employment opportunities.

## Positioning

Rifki is an end-to-end engineer who works across backend architecture, data modeling, APIs, frontend delivery, performance, reliability, and production operations. His record spans web products built since 2018 across e-commerce, retail and POS, fintech, cultural, automotive, and developer-tool contexts in Indonesia and international markets.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically scan the introduction, skills, work history, and selected projects; open the resume or live project links for deeper evidence; review GitHub or LinkedIn; and use email to start a conversation. The portfolio is a public, responsive website deployed at `rifkiramadhani.my.id`, with GitHub Pages base-path support retained by the project.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Present professional identity, experience, skills, and selected project work in one public portfolio.
- Link to the downloadable resume, live projects, public repositories when available, GitHub, LinkedIn, and email.
- Preserve repository-backed facts as the evidence boundary for future work. Do not invent testimonials, endorsements, clients, metrics, awards, certifications, pricing, or other proof.
- Keep unavailable repository links unpublished rather than implying that private or absent source code is public.
- Preserve the existing Astro deployment constraints, canonical-domain configuration, and support for both root and GitHub Pages base paths.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the professional name `Rifki Fajar Ramadhani` and role `Full-Stack Engineer`.
- Keep the voice direct, technically credible, collaborative, and grounded in shipped work rather than promotional claims.
- Treat the RF mark and lockup under `public/logo-mark.*` and `public/logo-lockup.*` as approved identity assets. Their visual treatment remains a design-system decision, not product truth.
- Preserve the current GitHub, LinkedIn, and email identities defined in `src/lib/site.ts` unless Rifki explicitly updates them.

## Evidence on Hand

- `public/resume.pdf` and `resume/resume.html` contain the detailed employment record and documented impact claims.
- `src/data/experience.ts` records roles across Indonesia, Singapore, and South Africa, including a documented improvement of data-loading performance by up to 50% at AutoWealth.
- `src/data/projects.ts` contains selected project descriptions, technology tags, live links, and repository links where public.
- Project screenshots under `public/` provide visual evidence for Typefn, Sportsmans Warehouse, Outdoor Warehouse, Shelflife, Museum MACAN, Woermann Fresh, Hey Halfway, Comprimage, and aibar.
- `src/data/skills.ts` records the current frontend and backend technology set.
- No testimonials, customer endorsements, awards, or independent case-study documents are currently on hand; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with shipped work and production responsibility.
2. Make credibility and relevant experience easy for hiring teams to scan.
3. Demonstrate end-to-end ownership without obscuring technical depth.
4. Prefer specific, repository-backed evidence over broad claims.
5. Keep the path from evaluation to resume, professional profiles, and contact direct.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve responsive access across mobile and desktop, keyboard-usable navigation and controls, semantic content, light and dark theme support, and reduced-motion behavior for visitors who request it.
